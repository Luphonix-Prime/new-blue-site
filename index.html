Frequently asked questions

What does Luphonix do?

At Luphonix, we help businesses transform ideas into powerful digital products through innovative technology and strategic development. From custom websites and mobile applications to AI-powered automation and enterprise software, we create scalable solutions that drive growth, improve efficiency, and deliver exceptional user experiences. Every project is built with modern technologies, performance, security, and long-term success in mind.

Technology shouldn't slow your business down—it should accelerate it. That's why we provide end-to-end support, including UI/UX design, cloud infrastructure, DevOps, API integration, and ongoing maintenance. Our team works closely with every client from planning to deployment, ensuring reliable, future-ready solutions tailored to their unique goals. Whether you're a startup building an MVP or an enterprise scaling digital operations, Luphonix is your trusted technology partner for innovation and growth.

What’s the time commitment during the program?

There are 5 tasks you need to do on day 1. These are important. From there, you’ll have a weekly 1-on-1 30-minute onboarding call (that you schedule during your free time) where we teach you exactly what you need to be doing. From there, you get weekly onboarding where we build off what you learned each week. That 30-minute call, plus 5 to 10 minutes per day (every day) is what’s expected from you.

Do you write all the email marketing for our business?

100%. Well, usually what we tell people is that we’ll write 95% of the emails, but we want you to go in and put your brand and voice spin on them. The hardest part of creating emails is just getting started. We do all the heavy lifting, you just finish the project.

Whats the typical Google Ads spend you suggest when getting started? 500$/mo is what we typically recommend. If you’re not willing to invest anything more than that, your money might be best spend elsewhere. Google needs a certain amount of money each month to properly learn and understand what you’re trying to convert for. 500$ to start seems to be the sweet spot.

How long until my system is up and running?

If you complete all your onboarding tasks, we can get the system built within the first month. The key is your onboarding. If you’re committed to the 1-on-1 calls and put in your 5-10 minutes of work a day, your system will be ready to go by the end of month 1.
